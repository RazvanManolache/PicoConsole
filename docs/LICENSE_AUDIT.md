# Licence and asset review

Reviewed source declarations, asset references and exclusions; not an independent ownership or trademark determination.

The combined firmware is distributed under GPLv3. Original component licences and copyright notices remain in force. No claim is made that unrelated upstream files are relicensed.

## Excluded from the public build

- `pad_eggs`: Handheld-game artwork has no separate provenance; conservatively excluded.
- `pad_flappy`: Imported 1984 commercial-game levels; permission not established.
- `pad_pacman`: Commercial-game logo, maze and recognisable artwork; separate permission not established.
- `pad_zoom`: Explicit original 1984 copyright and recreated content; permission not established.
- Sokoban: all imported puzzle collections and original intro map omitted. 128 original puzzles and solutions replace them, with every solution replay-verified. This also excludes Thinking Rabbit, Yoshio Murase, Loma, Domenech, du Peloux and sets with custom unchanged-only terms.
- Fifteen2: all picture puzzles omitted; only the numeric puzzle is built.
- Audio assets, commercial ROMs, upstream UF2s, unrelated sample programs and local Python packages are excluded from release bundles.
- The unused IBM bitmap font was removed from the port. The drawing fonts are selected from PicoLibSDK under its author declaration.

## Limits and release procedure

Review relies on upstream authors' declarations; it does not independently prove ownership of every sprite or clear game-name trademarks. The excluded recreations are conservative decisions, not findings that their authors infringed copyright. Submodule links alone do not discharge binary-source obligations: publish the matching self-contained source bundle generated by `tools/package_release.py` alongside each UF2. That bundle contains prepared, human-readable modified source and only required build dependencies; it does not copy all of PicoLibSDK.

Dependency revisions: `dependencies.lock.json`. Per-file hashes and per-game review: `logs/licence-audit.json`.

## Additional collections in 0.4

21 TinyJoypad additions and 27 Gamebuino additions are whitelisted by filename. Per-game authors, origins and terms are recorded in tools/tiny-games.json and tools/gamebuino-games.json. Original MIT, GPLv3, LGPLv3 and Unlicense texts/declarations are in LICENSES/TinyExtra-* and LICENSES/Gamebuino-*. Gamebuino API/fonts derive from Aurelien Rodot, LGPLv3-or-later; LGPL text and matching source are included.

The upstream collections contain other titles with missing, non-commercial, conflicting or incompatible declarations. They are not copied into the prepared firmware or release source archive. Built-in note tables may remain in source; separate audio files are omitted.

- gameStarHonor.c: Crashes in desktop input checks; original licence URL unavailable.
- gameElventure.c: Original repository/licence unavailable for independent confirmation.
- gameLightsOutAD.c: Original WTFPL attribution not independently retrievable; existing LightsOut retained.
- gameBangBang.c: Upstream incomplete: second player has no shooting behaviour; unsuitable for this handheld.
- gameFiremen.c: Upstream table states GPLv2; original licence file unavailable and GPLv3 compatibility not established.
- gameSnake5110.c: Upstream records conflicting CC-BY-SA / GPLv3 declarations.
- other Gamebuino titles: Missing or restrictive source declarations; only the reviewed filename whitelist is prepared.
- other TinyJoypad titles: Existing duplicates or missing/restrictive source declarations; only reviewed additions are prepared.
